Cookie Policy

Transparency about how we use cookies on our website

About This Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding their use. If you have further questions about the cookies we use, please get in touch with us at hello@switchcontractfurniture.co.uk or call 0333 210 1309.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or login details. Cookies are widely used across the internet to improve user experience and website functionality.

Your Cookie Choices

Google Analytics is optional. It is not loaded until you choose Accept analytics. Choosing Reject analytics lets you use the website without Google Analytics.

If you accept, Google Analytics helps us understand visits and page usage. It may set cookies whose names begin with _ga. We disable Google signals and advertising personalisation in our configuration.

Saving and Changing Your Choice

We save your choice in your browser's local storage under switch-analytics-choice-v1. This preference is used only to remember your analytics choice. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies to the current page only.

Use Cookie settings in the footer at any time to accept or reject analytics. Rejecting after accepting stops analytics on subsequent page loads, removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site and reloads the page. It does not delete information already sent to Google. Clearing your browser's site data also resets your choice.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to other websites. This Cookie Policy applies only to our website. We are not responsible for the cookie practices of external websites. When you visit another website through a link from our site, please review their cookie policy to understand how they use cookies.
