## Step 1 — Create the profile

1. Go to **https://www.google.com/business/** and click **Manage now**
2. Sign in with the Google account you want to own the profile (recommend: an account on the @switchworkplace.co.uk domain for longevity)
3. Search for *"Switch Contract Furniture"* — if it appears, claim it. If it doesn't, click **Add your business to Google**
4. Enter the business name exactly: **Switch Contract Furniture**

## Step 6 — Verification

Google will offer one or more verification methods depending on your account history:

- **Postcard (most common for new profiles)** — arrives at your Northallerton address in 5–14 days with a 5-digit code. Enter the code in the GBP dashboard to verify.
- **Phone** — occasionally offered; Google calls or texts a verification code
- **Email** — rarely offered for new business listings
- **Video verification** — sometimes required; you film a short video showing signage, the interior, and proof you run the business. This takes ~5 min.

**Do not use the listing aggressively until verified** — posting, responding to reviews etc. can wait. Verification is the gate.

## Step 8 — Ongoing (monthly)

After the profile is live, these are the highest-ROI maintenance activities:

1. **Post 1–2 updates per month** via the Updates tab — new product arrivals, completed projects, industry events, seasonal content. Posts keep the profile "fresh" in Google's eyes.
2. **Respond to every review within 48 hours** — both positive and negative. This is a massive trust signal.
3. **Ask 5 customers for reviews** in the first 90 days. Aim for 10+ reviews within 6 months. Send them this link (you'll get it from the dashboard): *bit.ly/switch-review* or similar short-link.
4. **Update hours** if there are bank holidays, closures, or seasonal changes
5. **Add new photos** every 1–2 months — new completed projects, new showroom setups, etc.

## Common pitfalls to avoid

- **Don't put keywords in the business name** (e.g. "Switch Contract Furniture - Northallerton Office Furniture Supplier"). Google will suspend listings that do this. Keep it exactly: *Switch Contract Furniture*.
- **Don't use a virtual office or mailbox address.** You're fine because you have a real office in Northallerton, but if you ever consider it, don't — Google rejects listings at co-working space addresses they can detect.
- **Don't set up multiple GBP listings for the same business** unless you have genuinely separate physical locations. One business = one listing.
- **Don't ignore negative reviews** — even a polite, professional response to a 1-star review improves how future customers perceive you.
- **Don't let the profile go stale.** A profile with no posts for 6 months ranks materially lower than an active one.
